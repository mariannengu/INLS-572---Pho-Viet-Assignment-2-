About the Chef

My father immigrated to the United States, seeking refuge from Vietnam when he was seven years old. He founded this restaurant out of a deep passion for sharing Vietnamese culture and cuisine with his community. Before pursuing this dream, he spent over a decade working in construction before transitioning to a career as a nail technician (Note: a lot of Vietnamese people end up as nail techs!!). Yet through it all, his true calling remained bringing the flavors and traditions of Vietnam to the Jacksonville and Wilmington area. What began with his first restaurant, Miss Pho Vietnamese Restaurant in Sneads Ferry, was never just a business venture. I would say that it was a labor of love and a testament to his immense pride in his heritage. He also constantly seeks to add more this menu!

I am incredibly proud of everything my father has built, and designing this website is my small way of honoring his hard work and dedication.
