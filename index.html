Welcome to Pho Viet

Experience authentic Vietnamese cuisine in the heart of Hampstead, North Carolina, just minutes from the beach. Our menu features traditional phở (pronounced /fə·\/ or "fuh"), flavorful rice dishes, fresh vermicelli bowls, handmade spring rolls, and much more.

In addition to our Vietnamese specialties, we offer a diverse selection of Asian, Chinese, Thai, and noodle dishes to satisfy every craving.

Whether you're looking for a relaxing dine-in experience or convenient carry-out, Pho Viet is your destination for delicious, authentic flavors.

04 Dietary Options

We try out best to offer a welcoming dining experience for our guests with various dietary needs. The restaurant's menu features several options that can accommodate different restrictions and preferences.

Our staff is happy to discuss ingredients modifications and preparation methods to ensure every one can enjoy an authentic Vietnamese meal.

Gluten-free/Gluten sensitive

Notice: We are still in the process of figuring out which of our menu items are gluten-free, so if you have a severe allergy I would be cautious of our other menu items.

(11-19.) Phở (Medium/Large)

Our pho is completely gluten-free because of its core ingredients of rice noodles, broth (made from beef bones and spices), meat, and fresh herbs. None of these contact wheat, but please avoid using hoisin sauce as it contains soy sauce.

05 FAQ

Below are some of our most frequently asked questions!

Q: Do you guys deliver?

A: No, we do not offer delivery at this time. We currently provide dine-in and carry-out options only.

Q: Are you guys hiring?

A: We are not hiring at this time, but we appreciate your interest and wish you the best in your job search.

Q: What items on your menu are vegetarian or gluten-free?

A: Please see our 'Dietary Options' page for a more complete list.

Q: What kind of soda do you have?

A: We do not serve fountain drinks. Instead we serve canned sodas including Coke, Diet Coke, Pepsi, Sprite, Mountain Dew, Dr. Pepper, and Fanta. This comes with a glass of ice for your convenience.

Q: Do you serve alcoholic beverages?

A: No, we do not but you are welcome to BYOB.
