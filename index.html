Welcome to Pho Viet

Experience authentic Vietnamese cuisine in the heart of Hampstead, North Carolina, just minutes from the beach. Our menu features traditional phở (pronounced /fə·\/ or "fuh"), flavorful rice dishes, fresh vermicelli bowls, handmade spring rolls, and much more.

In addition to our Vietnamese specialties, we offer a diverse selection of Asian, Chinese, Thai, and noodle dishes to satisfy every craving.

Whether you're looking for a relaxing dine-in experience or convenient carry-out, Pho Viet is your destination for delicious, authentic flavors.

04 Dietary Options

We're committed to accommodating various dietary needs and preferences. Please let us know about any restrictions when ordering.

Vegetarian Options

Many of our dishes can be prepared vegetarian upon request. We offer tofu substitutions and vegetable-based broths.

Gluten-Free

Rice noodles and many of our dishes are naturally gluten-free. Please inform your server of any gluten sensitivities.

Vegan Friendly

We can modify many dishes to be vegan. Ask about our vegetable broth options and plant-based proteins.

Allergen Information

Our menu contains common allergens including peanuts, shellfish, and soy. Please inform us of any allergies.

05 FAQ

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes! We offer convenient takeout service. Please call us at (910) 803-0479 to place your order. For delivery, check with popular delivery services in the area.

Do you take reservations?

We operate on a first-come, first-served basis. However, for large groups (6+), please call ahead so we can better accommodate you.

What is pho?

Phở (pronounced "fuh") is a traditional Vietnamese soup consisting of broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat. It's a beloved national dish of Vietnam known for its aromatic, flavorful broth.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Absolutely! We offer vegetarian versions of many dishes, including vegetable pho, tofu spring rolls, and vegetarian vermicelli bowls. Just let us know your preferences.

Do you serve alcohol?

We offer a selection of beer and wine to complement your meal. Please ask your server for our current beverage menu.
